How to Use

Full instructions and safety warnings are on the bottle label and in the Safety Data Sheet. A quick summary for planning:

  • PPE: Wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection.
  • Application: Pour directly into the urinal bowl or drain. Let it sit for the dwell time on the label so it can break down the scale.
  • Rinse: Flush thoroughly with water.
  • Cadence: Weekly for heavy-traffic restrooms, monthly for lighter use.

Do not mix with bleach or other cleaners. Keep out of reach of children.
